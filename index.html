Your teacher

Hannah Dunford

Yoga Mountain was founded in Wicklow in 2019 with one simple aim — to help everybody, at any age or stage, feel confident and at home in their own practice.

Hannah's teaching brings together pranayama, hatha flow, mindfulness and meditation, with a background in choral singing that surfaces as gentle mantra chanting. Trained as a Nutrition Specialist and in reflexology, massage, anatomy and physiology, she often folds therapeutic touch — temple massage with essential oils, neck and shoulder release — into a long savasana.

“My goal is constant and clear: that every student feels confident and empowered on their own journey toward a healthier, well-balanced life.”

Before yoga, Hannah spent more than a decade in the Irish music industry as a record label manager and tour manager, and years working with children — which is exactly why her kids' and teen classes land so well.

Concept rationale

What changed, and why

  1. A real first screenInstead of a page titled “Home” with a poster image, the hero says where you are, what's on and what to do next — in words, not baked into a JPEG.
  2. Text that Google can readClass times, retreat dates, prices and calls to action are live HTML, not text inside images. Searches for “yoga Enniskerry” can now find them.
  3. The timetable as a productDays, times, levels and eircodes in a scannable grid — the single thing most visitors came for.
  4. One retreat, sold properlyItinerary, price ladder and deposit terms in a sticky card, with the early-bird deadline visible.
  5. Warm, natural paletteSage, forest, oat paper and a saffron accent drawn straight from the existing logo — no linen wallpaper, no boxed 2012 theme frame.
  6. Type with a voiceA serif for headlines and a clean sans for reading, at a comfortable size and line length.
  7. Built mobile-firstFluid type, a real menu drawer, pinch-zoom no longer disabled, and dark-mode support.
  8. Accessibility & performanceProper heading order, described images, visible focus rings, reduced-motion support, and photos sized for the web.

All copy, photography and class details are the site's own, rearranged. Photography by The Visible Business.
